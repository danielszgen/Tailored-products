Hardware design

Electronics, mechanical and electromagnetics working together

Hardware design combines electronics development, mechanical architecture and multi-physics simulation to reduce uncertainty and improve product performance early in the process.

  • Electronics design with architecture focused on quality and robustness
  • Mechanical CAD, stress simulation and DFMEA-oriented development
  • Electromagnetic and thermal simulation for system-level optimization
  • Shorter development cycles with fewer physical prototypes

Firmware & software

Embedded intelligence for control, customization and usability

Firmware and software capabilities span architecture, validation, motor control and application tools, enabling flexible customer-facing solutions and robust internal development workflows.

  • Layered and modular software architecture
  • Motor control firmware for DC and brushless systems
  • Testing, code analysis and DevOps-oriented workflows
  • Tools for debugging, production, lab automation and customer configuration

Laboratory & validation

Validation capabilities that support reliable industrialization

Testing is not a separate step — it is a core capability integrated into product development to verify motors, electronics and software under real conditions.

  • Motors and electronics test benches
  • Data acquisition with CANoe, KVASER and LabView interfaces
  • CAN and LIN communication validation
  • External EMI, vibration and traction tests with DOGA follow-up
